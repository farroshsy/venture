Date committed: 2026-07-29, before any Phase 1.5 candidate was scored. This is
the single logged relaxation permitted after Phase 1's empty result (rule 6a):

## Hypothesis under test

Phase 1's central finding: where a universally accepted trust anchor already
exists — usually the state — blockchain almost never survives the kill test.

**H1 (testable form):** There exist, in or adjacent to the Indonesian economy,
trust failures where (a) no single institution is accepted as the anchor by
every economically relevant party (foreign verifiers, mutually adversarial
private parties, or custodians whose own integrity is the question), AND
(b) the failure supports a venture-scale company (≥7.0 under the rubric)
built as custodian-independent verification infrastructure.

Predictions, stated before scoring:
- **P1:** survivors, if any, concentrate in Axes 1, 2A, 2B, 3.
- **P2 (control):** Axis 5 candidates fail — a single sovereign can designate
  a canonical operator by decree (Perpres 94/2025 precedent), so kill tests
  2/3 bind.
- **Falsification:** an empty result across Axes 1–3 with adversarial memos
  executed means H1 is unsupported at venture scale in Indonesia; the project
  reverts to the Phase 1 option-B shapes or stop-and-monitor.

## Protocol upgrades from Phase 1 lessons

1. **Verification triggers widened.** An adversarial refutation memo is
   required for any candidate meeting ANY of: raw weighted ≥6.0; blockchain
   necessity ≥7 (the dimension most vulnerable to optimistic reasoning,
   regardless of total); competition ≥7 (whitespace claims are unproven
   without a memo). Phase 1's single-memo outcome (trigger ≥6.5) left the
   gold-bullion whitespace claim untested.
2. **Round 1 leftover executed:** the gold-bullion adversarial memo is
   commissioned in this run.
3. **Bar unchanged:** 7.0 after single-judge normalization. The Phase 1 empty
   shortlist is treated as evidence, not as a reason to move the bar.
4. **COLL discipline carried forward:** any sizing resting on off-system
   collusion losses is re-scored with those stripped.
