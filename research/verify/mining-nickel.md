## What I found

### 1. Indonesian / SEA startups: none new

No Indonesian or SEA startup doing mining provenance surfaced beyond Baliola, which remains an NFT-marketplace-pedigree outfit (Mandala Chain, gov MOUs e.g. Jabar, Mar 2025) with no mining deployment or funding news found through mid-2026. Koltiva stays agri-only (rubber/cocoa/palm). No Minespider/MineHub/Optel Indonesian nickel deployments found. Circulor was quiet in 2025–26 news (last notable: Deloitte alliance 2023). This leg does NOT refute.

### 2. Government systems: actively absorbing the layer

- **Perpres 94/2025** embeds SIMBARA optimization in commodity governance; SIMBARA covers 5 commodities in 2026 (DDTC, Nov 2025).
- **Kemenko Maritim, May 28, 2026:** "Hilirisasi Komoditas Nikel dan Timah Siap Implementasi Melalui SIMBARA" — SIMBARA extending into downstream (smelter-side) tracking.
- **CNBC Indonesia op-ed, Jul 28, 2026** ("SIMBARA, Traceability, dan Masa Depan Green Economy Indonesia") by Mochamad Agus Rofiudin, expert staff to the Finance Minister for PNBP: frames SIMBARA as Indonesia's **digital chain of custody** across people/commodity/document/logistics/financial flows, explicitly aligning to **Battery Passport / Digital Product Passport**. Also confirms surveyor LHV reports already feed SIMBARA (the "accepted intermediary" — IDSurvey — is wired in). Admits gaps: real-time RKAB data, interoperability, data quality.
- **Luhut, May 25, 2026:** AI-based export surveillance layered on SIMBARA (World Bank consulted); and **Danantara Sumberdaya Indonesia (DSI)** one-door export regime for coal, with miners publicly worried DSI overlaps SIMBARA (Bloomberg Technoz, Jul 24, 2026). The state is moving to own the entire export trust chain — referee and player.

### 3. Incumbent verification: EU compliance is running through audit schemes, not pit-custody software

- **Harita Nickel is the first Indonesian nickel company under RMAP+ audit** (per the Jul 2026 MoF op-ed).
- **PT Vale Indonesia entered stage-two IRMA audit** (vale.com, Dec 30, 2025); investor pressure documented (Reuters, Apr 8, 2026).
- EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542 due diligence (Arts. 48–52, Annex X) requires OECD-aligned due-diligence policies verified by notified bodies; recognized schemes (RMAP, IRMA) are the practical compliance path. The battery passport's sourcing attributes are due-diligence-report level — **no cryptographic pit-level custody proof is mandated**. GBA wave-two passport pilots (Nov 2024, incl. two CATL programs) sit at the OEM/cell end.
- Nickel Industries' sustainability head (Media Nikel Indonesia, Dec 2025) discusses battery-passport strategy entirely in audit/ISO terms; no traceability vendor named.

### 4. Single trusted operator test

Domestically: SIMBARA + IDSurvey surveyors (LHV already integrated) + prospectively DSI one-door exports = a state-run trusted-operator stack that is being built now. The dokumen terbang fraud persisting into 2026 is a pit-level enforcement and collusion problem — bribed surveyors and complicit smelters poison any ledger at ingestion ("garbage in, chain of garbage"), and the marginal ore buyer benefits from ambiguity, so the natural payer is the fraud's beneficiary. For the EU side, notified bodies accept scheme audits; they do not demand custodian-independent cryptographic proof.

## Verdict: REFUTED (as claimed)

Two of the phase-1 memo's own kill conditions are now evidenced as happening: (a) EU compliance being satisfied by paper/scheme audits — Harita RMAP+, Vale IRMA — which drains the "mandated spend needs verified pit origin by Feb 2027" wedge; (b) SIMBARA expanding toward the chain-of-custody/battery-passport layer itself (Perpres 94/2025, hilirisasi implementation, AI monitoring, DSI one-door exports). No new private competitor was found — the space is empty of startups — but it is empty because the buyer path is closing: the state claims the layer, the compliance budget flows to audit schemes, and the domestic fraud is an enforcement problem, not a database problem. Competition score of 5/10 was too generous to the gap; the venture-scale claim collapses.

**Surviving gap (niche):** independent pit-level ESG evidence — satellite imagery x license polygons x carbon intensity — sold as audit support to OEMs, RMAP/IRMA auditors, and notified bodies. Service-heavy, auditor-adjacent, not the claimed pit-to-passport platform.
