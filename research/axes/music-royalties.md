# Music Royalty Collection & Distribution — axis 3

## Problem & Current Workflow

Under UU 28/2014 and PP 56/2021, performing-rights royalties in Indonesia are collected centrally by LMKN (Lembaga Manajemen Kolektif Nasional), a state-chartered body. Workflow today: commercial users — restaurants, hotels, karaoke, broadcasters, concert promoters — pay blanket tariffs to LMKN; LMKN allocates the pool to member LMKs (WAMI and others for authors; performer/producer LMKs for related rights), which pay their members. Usage data is the weak link: analog venues rarely submit playlists, so distribution historically leaned on estimates and membership counts; LMKN itself admits past distribution "lacked adequate data support and did not have a standard, transparent distribution scheme" (LMKN, 2025). The SILM (Sistem Informasi Lagu dan/atau Musik) mandated by PP 56/2021 to log actual usage is still being stood up; LMKN's 2025-2028 plan centers on "comprehensive digital transformation" (LMKN, 2025). Digital DSP royalties partly flow through LMKN (Rp125.87 billion distributed in 2025) with DSP-supplied logs the rights holders cannot independently audit.

## Stakeholders & Who Pays (both sides — partially cross-border)

Domestic: songwriters and performers (beneficiaries), LMKs (WAMI et al.), LMKN commissioners, commercial users who pay tariffs, Kemenkum (regulator, now with a Tim Pengawas LMKN/LMK under Permenkum 27/2025), DPR (holding hearings), and courts. Foreign: DSPs (Spotify, Apple, YouTube — custodians of the usage logs that determine digital splits) and overseas CMOs, whose reciprocal flows LMKN books as "overseas" royalties (Rp27.94 billion distributed 2025, LMKN 2026). Operationally the users pay; economically the songwriters pay via leakage; LMKN's operating cost is now capped at 8% of collections (Permenkum 27/2025).

## Trust Breakdown (cited incidents)

- 9 June 2026: songwriters and musicians protested at LMKN's Jakarta office demanding commissioners resign, immediate distribution, cancellation of Circular 06/LMKN/VIII-2025, and return of collection/distribution functions to the individual LMKs (Metro TV News, 2026) — the custodian's own integrity contested in the street.
- Agnez Mo v. Ari Bias: PN Jakarta Pusat ordered Rp1.5 billion (30 January 2025) for three concerts performed without the songwriter's direct license; the Supreme Court granted cassation and annulled it (2025); Ari Bias re-sued four parties for Rp4.9 billion in December 2025 (Hukumonline 2025; CNN Indonesia 2025). The case split the industry over whether LMKN's blanket license even settles the obligation — i.e., whether the anchor legally anchors anything.
- Unclaimed royalties accumulated to Rp70.44 billion through 2025 (Rp54.39 billion digital), and LMKN's first-ever public unclaimed announcement (Rp33.02 billion since 2021) came only under ministry pressure (LMKN 2026; Kemenkum Bali 2026).
- DPR RDPU 20-21 November 2025: musicians (Once Mekel) demanded interconnected digital systems for LMKN and LMKs; vendor VNT Networks presented PDLM/SILM technology; no decision taken (detik, 2025).

## Cost of the Problem

LMKN collected over Rp200 billion (~US$12M) in 2025 — analog Rp77.88 billion — and distributed Rp170.98 billion total, of which Rp151.83 billion reached 16,332 rights holders via LMKs (LMKN, 2026). Stuck value: Rp70.44 billion unclaimed (LMKN, 2026); Rp36.4 billion pending for July-December 2025 (Metro TV News, 2026). Litigation exposure is real but idiosyncratic (Rp1.5-4.9 billion claims in the Ari Bias saga). Strip: leakage from venues that simply don't pay is enforcement failure, not verification failure, and no source separates the two (unverified). The entire national performance-royalty pool is roughly one mid-size US venue's ad budget — the money is small even if the anger is large.

## Existing Solutions (as of 2026-07)

- Permenkum 27/2025 (implementing PP 56/2021): >20 covered service types, 8% opex cap, ministry-level supervisory team — the state's governance answer, in force (Kemenkum, 2025).
- SILM/PDLM: usage-data systems specified by regulation; VNT Networks pitching as technology provider (detik, 2025); LMKN's "Inspirasi" portal for rights-holder data.
- LMKN's 2026 distribution decree (SK 001.SK.LMKN.IV.2026) shifting to data-quality-based formulas (LMKN, 2026).
- Global: fingerprinting/monitoring vendors (BMAT-style venue monitoring; presence in Indonesia unverified). Blockchain royalty ventures globally have largely failed or pivoted — Utopia Music's collapse and peers could not be re-verified within this session's search budget (unverified, flagged).

## Why Insufficient & Who Rejects the Existing Anchor

This is the closest H1 fit of the three: the designated anchor (LMKN) is rejected by its own beneficiaries. Songwriters dispute its numbers; LMKs want their functions back; a songwriter (Ari Bias) rejected the blanket-license anchor outright via direct licensing and litigated; users don't know whom paying actually discharges. Meanwhile LMKN itself cannot verify the upstream logs — foreign DSPs and analog venues custody the usage data. Yet the rejection points toward auditability, not custodian-independence: what protesters and Once Mekel demand is a working, transparent, state-audited digital system, and the ministry is delivering exactly that by decree. No economically relevant party is demanding proof independent of every custodian.

## Blockchain Test

Test 1 passes — the mess predates blockchain. Test 2: marginal fail for the thesis — a well-run central system under ministry audit would satisfy the domestic parties; the DSP-log problem is a data-access problem a ledger cannot fix without DSP participation. Test 3 fails hard: the state is shipping governance by decree right now (Permenkum 27/2025, supervisory team, 2026 distribution SK), and SILM procurement is underway with VNT Networks circling. Test 4 fails decisively: ~US$12M annual collections with an 8% opex cap leaves ~US$1M/year for all administration — no standalone company fits inside that.

## Kill Test Verdict

KILLED — by economics (test 4) and state decree velocity (test 3), despite genuinely contested trust (the best chain score of this batch). H1 lesson: a contested anchor is necessary but not sufficient; the pool must also be large enough to fund independent verification.

## What Would Kill This Idea

Already killed by market size and the decree in progress. Revival conditions: digital cross-border royalty flows (DSP-to-Indonesia) growing an order of magnitude, foreign CMOs refusing LMKN's numbers for reciprocal settlement, and SILM failing publicly after Permenkum 27/2025 — i.e., the state anchor burning its second chance.
